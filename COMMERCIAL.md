# Commercial licensing

MediaTrove is released under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 only
(AGPL-3.0-only)**. That license already allows commercial activity — but it
requires you to pass on the same freedoms: if you distribute the software (or a
modified version of it), or let users interact with a modified version over a
network, you must publish the complete corresponding source under the AGPL.

## When a commercial license is needed

Buy a commercial license if you cannot or do not want to meet those
obligations, for example:

- shipping MediaTrove (or a modified/rebranded build) inside a paid product,
  device, or app store listing without releasing your source;
- running it as (part of) a hosted/SaaS offering where users interact with your
  modified version remotely, without publishing your changes;
- embedding it in an internal product where the AGPL's source-sharing terms are
  incompatible with your company policy or your own customers' licenses.

Not needed — the AGPL already covers these for free, as long as you comply with
it:

- personal use, study, research, teaching and non-profit projects;
- running it yourself, unmodified or modified, without offering it to others as
  a network service;
- forking it publicly as an open-source project under the AGPL (that is exactly
  what the license is for).

## How to obtain one

Open an issue on <https://github.com/liumeteor11/mediatrove/issues> describing:

1. your company / product and how MediaTrove would be used;
2. whether you distribute it, host it, or both;
3. the scale (internal users, end customers, devices).

A separate written commercial license — typically per-product or per-seat —
will then be agreed.

## Why dual licensing is possible

Dual licensing requires the licensor to hold the copyright (or an explicit
right to relicense) for the whole work. This repository is maintained by the
copyright holder; contributions are accepted under the terms that allow the
maintainer to offer them under both the AGPL and a commercial license.
